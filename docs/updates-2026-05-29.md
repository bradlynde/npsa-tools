# Sales Toolbox — Product Updates

**Release date:** May 29, 2026
**Audience:** NPSA Sales Team
**Purpose:** Summary of new capabilities for team awareness and SOP updates.

---

## Overview

The tool formerly known as the **LOE Generator** has been expanded into the
**Sales Toolbox** — a single home for generating client-facing documents and
preparing for calls. Three new capabilities were added, and the dashboard was
reorganized to make everything easier to find.

The dashboard is now grouped into three sections:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Engagement Letters** | Generate New Letter, Load Previous Letter, plus team stats & Rep Leaderboard |
| **Proposals & Addendums** | New Proposal, New Addendum |
| **Tools** | Pre-Call Notes Generator *(In Beta)* |

---

## 1. Proposal Generator *(New)*

### What it is
A clean, 1–2 page executive **proposal** that summarizes scope and price for a
prospect's leadership team. It is a presentation-friendly view of the same
information that goes into a pre-award engagement letter — built for decision
makers who want the highlights, not the full legal document.

### When to use it
- When a prospect's board, finance lead, or executive director wants a concise
  overview before committing to the full engagement letter.
- Early in the sales conversation, to put scope and price in front of leadership.

### What's included in the document
- NPSA letterhead and a **Client | Consultant** info block (matches the
  engagement letter design).
- A summary box: **Project**, **Potential Funding**, and **Professional Fee**.
- **Executive Summary** paragraph.
- **Project Locations** (pulled from the locations you enter in the sidebar).
- **Three-Phase Approach**: Pre-Award, Compliance Period, and Award
  Implementation. Award Implementation is presented as **optional and under a
  separate agreement** — never as something included in this engagement.
- **Eligible Project Types**.
- **Investment & Payment Terms** (pulled live from the fee engine, including any
  early-signing discount and deadline).
- A closing note clarifying that the Engagement Letter is the controlling
  document.

### How to use it
1. From the Sales Toolbox dashboard, click **New Proposal**.
2. Fill in the sidebar exactly as you would for a pre-award letter (client info,
   locations, programs, and pricing). The proposal reuses these inputs.
3. Review the live preview, then print / save as PDF.

> **Note:** Proposals can be saved and reloaded like letters, but they do **not**
> count toward the Total Letters or Rep Leaderboard stats.

## 2. Compliance Addendum Generator *(New)*

### What it is
A document that **removes premature Implementation Period (post-award) services**
from an engagement letter that has already been signed. Grant program rules
prohibit contracting for implementation services before award compliance steps
are complete, so this addendum cleanly cancels those services and fees from the
original agreement.

### When to use it
- When a client has already signed an engagement letter that included
  post-award implementation/management services, and those services need to be
  removed to stay compliant with grant program rules.

### What's included in the document
- NPSA letterhead and a **Client | Consultant** info block (auto-populated from
  the client and contact details).
- Five standard sections: Purpose, Mutual Agreement to Remove Implementation
  Period Services, No Obligation to Enter a New Agreement, Preservation of
  Remaining Terms, and Intent & Good Faith.
- A signature block with the client's name and title pre-filled.
- Language tailored to the specific grant program(s) involved.

### How to use it
1. From the Sales Toolbox dashboard, click **New Addendum**.
2. In the sidebar, enter/confirm the **client name**, the **original agreement
   date**, and the **grant program(s)** the addendum applies to.
3. Confirm the contact name/title (used in the signature block) and the NPSA
   signer.
4. Review the preview, then print / save as PDF for signature.

> **Note:** Addendums can be saved and reloaded, but they do **not** count toward
> the Total Letters or Rep Leaderboard stats.

## 3. Pre-Call Notes Generator *(New — In Beta)*

### What it is
An AI-powered tool that turns a **Calendly invite** into structured prep notes
for an upcoming call. It reads the meeting details and researches the
organization's website to help confirm attendee titles and campus addresses.

### When to use it
- Before a discovery or sales call, to walk in prepared with context on the
  organization and attendees.

### How to use it
1. From the Sales Toolbox dashboard, open **Pre-Call Notes Generator** (in the
   Tools section).
2. Paste the **full Calendly invite email** into the box.
3. Click **Generate Notes**. The AI will research and produce structured prep
   notes.
4. Use **Copy to Clipboard** or **Print / Save as PDF** to keep them.

> **In Beta:** This feature is still being refined. The AI researches the org's
> public website on a best-effort basis. Anything it can't verify is marked
> **TBD** for you to confirm manually — always double-check titles and addresses
> before the call.

## 4. Dashboard & Experience Improvements

- **Renamed to "Sales Toolbox"** to reflect that it now holds more than just
  engagement letters.
- **Reorganized into three clearly labeled sections** (Engagement Letters,
  Proposals & Addendums, Tools) so each tool is easy to find.
- **Animated stat counters** — the Total Letters and Total Fees figures roll up
  like a counter when the dashboard loads and when you hover over them.
- **Consistent document branding** — proposals and addendums now share the same
  professional header and layout as the engagement letters.
